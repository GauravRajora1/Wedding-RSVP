Our Story

The Window Seat
That Changed Everything

Gaurav deliberately picked the window seat on a midnight flight to the UK — 13th September 2023, 00:15, both of them leaving home for the first time to study abroad. Saloni hadn't picked any seat at all. She was simply placed right next to him. That was the first time he saw her, and somehow, he even has a photo from the exact moment they met.

It really was magical. Somewhere over the clouds, it felt like leaving one hand behind and grabbing another — the start of something neither of them expected mid-flight.

We went to different universities in the UK but stayed inseparable — study sessions, shopping trips, and everything in between. Within a month of that flight, Gaurav proposed. She said YES. We're both the first children in our families to ever move abroad, and we've been navigating every "first" together ever since.
